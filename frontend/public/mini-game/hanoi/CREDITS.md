## In-repository illustrations

The blank cup, geometric cup, leaf-pattern cup and neutral lunch table are rendered from SVG code in `src/features/mini-game/components/StoryCard.tsx`. They are illustrative interface artwork, not photographs or evidence of a real pottery service. No cultural significance is assigned to the patterns, and the images do not imply firing or immediate collection.

The photographs are contextual illustrations. In particular, the cafe photograph does not identify the fictional cafe, imply endorsement, or confirm current opening hours/prices. Source-page links are also available next to the photos in the experience and its journal.
